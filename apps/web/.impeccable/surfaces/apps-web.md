# Surface brief: Kantim web app + landing (Obolo)

Scope: apps/web (Operate) and apps/landing (Persuade), full redesign into the user-pinned "Obolo" language. packages/ui carries the shared tokens and primitives.

Audience and job: members of one home check what needs attention and log money (app); a person running a home decides to sign up or self-host (landing). Proof is the product itself; demo data is labeled as an example; no invented claims.

Resolved decisions: outflow amounts in ink with a real minus, peach only in graphics; account cards map the account color to the nearest card gradient, no invented card numbers; the command palette is navigation plus quick actions. Wordmark "kantim." keeps its lockup with a coral period. Craft-floor eyebrow ban: page headers carry a muted context sentence (date, counts), never an uppercase kicker; uppercase labels only head list groups.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private bank's ledger for the home. Numbers are the hero, every card explains itself, and hierarchy comes from type weight, not color. Refuses the dense admin-table dashboard and the neon fintech look.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas with mint and blush radial washes; deep forest green primary; a floating dark sidebar in both themes; 20px cards with barely-there shadows (borders in dark); pill buttons and chips; pastel data palette (green in, peach out, blue, pink, olive, slate, orange); Geist with tabular numerals, Geist Mono for identifiers only.

STORY: The member sees what is going out soon, whether the home can cover it, and which limit runs ahead of pace, then acts from a drawer. The landing visitor sees that same home screen working and believes one calm place can replace the spreadsheet and the fridge notes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App: floating sidebar left, transparent top bar (pill search with a command-K chip, a live month-net pill, round icon tools, "New transaction" pill), a context line and a 40px H1, a KPI strip, then the projected-balance stepped forecast ("Can we cover it?") beside a right rail. The brief's diverging in/out chart is deferred: no endpoint returns monthly in/out history and the web may not compute it. Landing: a large H1 and lead with pill CTAs on the left, and a live-built Kantim home screen (sidebar, KPI strip, Going out soon) floating on the washed canvas on the right.
